00 STRUCTURAL ENGINE

OpenSees structural response

  • Import building inventory and earthquake records.
  • Assign record scale factors, generate Tcl models and run OpenSees.
  • Collect periods, displacement, drift and damage classifications.
DECISION OUTPUT

Structural evidence package and a controlled bridge into Phase 1.

01 FUNCTIONAL LOSS

Building-to-portfolio functionality

  • Assess building-level and regional functionality.
  • Classify points of interest and aggregate by occupancy.
  • Resolve single-function, multi-function and unified portfolio results.
DECISION OUTPUT

Service-loss hotspots and comparable portfolio performance evidence.

02 PEOPLE & EMERGENCY NEEDS

Human impact under cold conditions

  • Combine building risk, occupancy, infrastructure and temperature-adjusted evacuation.
  • Estimate casualties, usability, shelter, heating and medical demand.
  • Aggregate regional results and compare the built-in code baseline where applicable.
DECISION OUTPUT

A people-centred emergency-demand picture for response planning.

03 RECOVERY & RESILIENCE

Ten-level recovery chain

  • Connect building attributes with infrastructure and management conditions.
  • Aggregate POI, single-function and multi-function recovery times.
  • Produce scheduling, recovery curves and final resilience results with life-rescue priorities.
DECISION OUTPUT

Restoration sequence, recovery time and resilience index for scenario review.

04 BRB-ER SMART REPORTS

Traceable decision reporting

  • Check that required phase results exist before a final report is released.
  • Generate structural, functional-loss, people-impact and recovery report views.
  • Package BRB-ER interpretation, recommendations and evidence for Word, PDF and Excel export.
DECISION OUTPUT

A traceable briefing package for technical and executive review.

05 3D CITY DECISION VIEWER

Report-linked urban intelligence

  • Transfer the latest city-ready Phase 1–3 report data through Smart Check / Build.
  • Switch building and regional views across functionality, people needs, recovery and priorities.
  • Inspect, filter, rotate, fit and export the city view plus merged Excel/CSV data.
DECISION OUTPUT

A spatial brief for prioritization, coordination and stakeholder communication.

BP-CRERE reads the completed phase summaries, translates phase-specific result severity into belief evidence and uses an Evidential Reasoning layer to form a traceable decision interpretation.

Stakeholder questions

Clarity before commitment.

Does BP-CRERE replace an existing emergency-management system?+

No. A pilot should define how BP-CRERE complements existing systems, data governance and authority structures.

Can the platform be configured for another country?+

The workflow is designed for localization, but every implementation requires country-specific data mapping, engineering validation and institutional review.

Can it support limited-connectivity environments?+

The platform includes online and offline workflow options. Deployment details should be assessed against the organization’s infrastructure and security requirements.

Is BP-CRERE ready for unsupervised operational decisions?+

No. It is a research-backed decision-support platform. Outputs require professional review and authorization by the responsible local institution.
