Professional profile

Research built on engineering depth and usable outcomes.

My work advances resilient buildings and urban systems by combining structural engineering, artificial intelligence and uncertainty-aware reasoning.

During my PhD at Harbin Institute of Technology, I have investigated post-earthquake building function, building portfolios, urban casualty estimation, recovery, resilience and progressive collapse.
